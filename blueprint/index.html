On attention heads and bilinear forms

Andrew O’Desky

Project home  \(\cdot \)  Dependency graph

This blueprint presents the mathematical results implemented in the audited Lean formalization of On attention heads and bilinear forms. It covers Appendices A, B and C, together with Definition 6 and Theorem 5 from the main text. Lemma, corollary, proposition and theorem numbers match the paper; auxiliary definitions are named separately.

Each result includes a proof outline and links to its Lean declarations. The dependency graph records the mathematical dependencies between these results and definitions; internal helper lemmas and mathlib dependencies are omitted. A green result and proof indicate that both have been formalized. The source links point to the audited revision of the Lean files.

The empirical observations and the other main-text theorems are outside this formalization. In particular, Lemma 7 is formalized here, but the main-text accumulation theorem that applies it is not. The two expository remarks following Lemma 11 are also outside the scope.
