Shapes of attention heads

Interactive figures for On attention heads and bilinear forms.

Every attention head carries a bilinear form L = WKTWQ on the residual stream. Write it as L = S + T with S symmetric and T antisymmetric, and scale so that ‖L‖ = 1. Let λ+ list the positive eigenvalues of S and λ− the absolute values of its negative eigenvalues, both decreasing and padded with zeros to a common length. The shape of L is

a = ‖T‖2,   b = 2 ⟨λ+, λ−⟩,   c = ‖(λ+ − λ−)+‖2,   d = ‖(λ+ − λ−)−‖2,

taking positive and negative parts entrywise. These are nonnegative and sum to one, so the shape is a point of the 3‑simplex, drawn as a tetrahedron whose vertices are the antisymmetric forms (a = 1), the forms whose two lobes agree (b = 1), and the positive and negative semidefinite forms (c = 1, d = 1).

Each page below plots the shape of every head of one model, coloured by spectral type. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and hover a point for the layer and head it belongs to. Use the model dropdown at the top to switch models. The tabs at the bottom select the 3-D simplex, the 2-D projection to Δ, parity profiles (k = 3), or clusters by layer (k = 3).
